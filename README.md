## 📖 Overview

**UNDERGROUNDCITY** is a next-generation **GeoAI decision-support platform** for identifying optimal locations for underground city development across Malaysia. It combines **live satellite data**, **seismic records**, **geological formations**, and **multi-criteria spatial analysis** to rank candidate sites — all with an interactive **cyberpunk-styled interface**.

Whether you're an urban planner, geologist, researcher, student, or simply curious about subterranean infrastructure, this tool gives you **real data-driven insights** in seconds.

## 🎯 The 10 Scoring Criteria

Every site is evaluated across **10 weighted criteria** for underground city suitability:

| # | Criterion | Weight | Description |
|---|-----------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | 🏔️ **Elevation** | 15% | Higher = safer from flooding |
| 2 | 🪨 **Bedrock Depth** | 15% | Shallow bedrock = easier excavation |
| 3 | 💧 **Water Table Depth** | 12% | Deep = less groundwater issues |
| 4 | 🗿 **Geology** | 12% | Karst / granite formations ideal |
| 5 | 🏙️ **Population Density** | 10% | Low = abundant cheap land |
| 6 | 🛣️ **Infrastructure Access** | 10% | Airports, roads, ports nearby |
| 7 | 🌋 **Seismic Risk** | 8% | From live USGS earthquake data |
| 8 | 🌊 **Coastal Proximity** | 8% | Balanced access vs. sea-level risk |
| 9 | 🌳 **Land Availability** | 5% | Forest vs. developed areas |
| 10 | 🌊 **Flood Risk** | 5% | Derived from elevation |

**Penalties applied** for critical factors (low elevation, high flood risk, dense population, shallow water table, high seismic risk, deep bedrock).
